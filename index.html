ABOUT THE FOUNDER

From Automating CSR Programmes to Leading AI-Driven Web Engineering

Usama Abu Bakr is the CEO and Founder of Kraken Code. His career began in corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, where he ran programmes held back by endless manual reporting, outreach and coordination.

Determined to remove that friction, he turned to artificial intelligence — first automating CSR workflows, then studying the systems behind them. One pattern kept surfacing: organisations were limited not by ideas, but by websites and tooling that took too long and cost too much to build. He went deep on AI-driven development efficiency, engineering disciplined workflows in which AI carries the heavy lifting under strict engineering control — cutting delivery from months to days without sacrificing quality.

Today Usama leads Kraken Code across three disciplines: AI implementation inside business operations, AI-driven web development, and lead-generation engines. He remains hands-on with every engagement — architecting, reviewing and shipping the work himself.
